Buy to let mortgages

Purchasing a property as an investment requires a different approach to residential lending. Buy to let mortgages are assessed based on rental income as well as your personal financial position, which is why tailored advice is essential.

Book a call01246 551 155

Whether you're exploring buy to let for the first time or expanding an existing portfolio, specialist guidance can help you understand lender expectations, deposit requirements, and potential returns.

Please note: not all buy to let mortgages are regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.
